## 1. Character-led educational series

- Supplied: https://pin.it/1g88dHkH2
- Resolved: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/610026712068886619/
- Page title: “Creative Social Media Carousel Design”; Brandlite Consulting artwork, saved by IJAY. Linked source: https://www.instagram.com/p/DcBRVVSitFc/
- Inspected: cover, a content slide, closing slide, and five-slide thumbnail strip.
- Observed: deep green field, lime emphasis, large white “5,” an expressive cropped 3D character at the right edge, small brand/header details. A content slide uses a lime numbered heading, white explanation, and character fragments at the left and lower right. The closing slide moves the figure left, places the proposition to the right, and introduces a bright CTA panel.
- Transfer: the character provides continuity while its scale and position change; a giant numeral creates hierarchy; the CTA changes composition while retaining the visual world.
- Adapt: use a client's primary dark environment and contrasting emphasis; an original character or real branded product can carry the recurring role.
- Improve when applying: the reference's fine footer and dense body text should not be copied at an unreadable mobile size. Do not force a mascot onto brands that do not suit one.

## 2. Photographic subject with floating content panels

- Supplied: https://pin.it/3rS5OGO6y
- Resolved: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/290130401020740620/
- Page title: “Found on Cosmos”; description attributes the post to Mobile Editing Club. Linked source: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1170915177
- Inspected: one visible cover poster; no full slide sequence was exposed on this pin.
- Observed: a woman photographed outdoors among grasses, blue sky, floating translucent social-post panels in perspective, very large centered white headline spanning the subject, a small top identity, and a restrained dark CTA pill near the bottom.
- Transfer: recognizable human subject + oversized type + peripheral depth establishes a campaign world. Panels frame the subject rather than replacing it.
- Adapt: use authorized brand photography or an original illustrative scene; apply the brand accent to small controls and supporting panels. Keep believable lighting and a protected text region.
- Improve when applying: simplify floating panels on teaching slides; miniature interface text should not carry essential information. This pin's cover does not establish how the unseen content slides were designed.

## 3. Editorial photography in a receding stack

- Supplied: https://pin.it/7J15b7K4t

- Page title: “ChatGPT,” description “Carousel System”; saved by Clean Pages Club - PPT Templates. The actual artwork reads “03 URBAN CULTURE.”

- Observed: charcoal background; a central rectangular image of a man with hands raised; photographs above and below recede with perspective and soft blur; narrow tall white type and “03” at the left; sparse, widely spaced small metadata at the edges.

## 5. Let's Corn: bold comparison education

- Supplied: https://pin.it/3YLF6XIfi
- Resolved: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1149754979889511915/
- Page title: “Pin on Social”; creator shown as Let's Corn. Linked source: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWUCC5tDG2N/
- Inspected: cover, a stable full view of the third image, and eight-image thumbnail strip.
- Observed: near-black cover, photographed woman, huge metallic-looking “5,” bold white “VS,” purple tilted word labels, compact brand pills. The third image uses a centered two-line headline with purple emphasis, two white example panels labeled “Boring” and “Viral,” disordered grey text shapes on the left and an aligned reading path on the right, a lunar surface crop at lower left, a progress pill above, and a small logo below. Thumbnails show alternation among examples, bold typographic messages, and CTA compositions.
- Transfer: strong scale contrast and a repeatable opposition make the promise quickly scannable; accent labels communicate the relationship rather than covering every surface.

- Improve when applying: the creator's caption discusses “viral” outcomes, but the artwork does not prove performance. Never turn that into an engagement guarantee. Use a moon or other metaphor only when relevant.

- Page title: “Innovative Instagram & LinkedIn Carousel Designs for Social Media Success”; saved by Aesthetic Font Studio. Artwork is signed Asad Ali / @freelancingwithasad. The linked Behance destination describes fonts; its content was not used as evidence.
- Inspected: one visible cover poster.
- Observed: black cinematic background; large red superhero crop on the right, extending off-frame; condensed uppercase white “CAROUSEL” and turquoise “FONTS”; a tilted small “PERFECT” stamp; dashed curved arrow toward “6 COMBINATIONS”; small author signature at the bottom.
- Transfer: a strong silhouette, tall condensed display type, one bright accent, and a secondary stamp create an immediate reading order.

- Improve when applying: use cinematic impact selectively, especially on the cover. Body slides need a quieter reading system. Do not copy the character or author signature.

## Synthesis: what the skill takes from the set

The common thread is deliberate hierarchy and a coherent visual world: large type or numerals, one dominant subject, controlled accents, purposeful depth, and small consistent brand anchors. The set spans restrained editorial imagery and expressive 3D/character work. Choose among them according to the client rather than treating neon accents, dark fields, characters, metallic finishes, or 3D objects as universal requirements.

The numerical sizing defaults, contrast checks, output schema, and review process in SKILL.md are practical production recommendations added by this skill; they were not measured from the pins.
